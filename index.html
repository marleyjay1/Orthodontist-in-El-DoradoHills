Comprehensive Orthodontic Treatment Options

Every smile is different, which is why orthodontic treatment should be personalised. Modern orthodontics offers several options to address common alignment and bite concerns.

Traditional Metal Braces

Metal braces remain a reliable option for correcting various orthodontic issues. They use brackets and wires to gradually move teeth into their desired positions. Traditional braces can address complex alignment problems, overcrowding, gaps, and bite irregularities.

Clear Braces

Clear braces offer a more discreet alternative to traditional metal braces. They work using a similar system but feature tooth-coloured or transparent brackets that blend more naturally with your smile. This option is popular among teenagers and adults who prefer a less noticeable orthodontic treatment.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are removable trays designed to gradually reposition teeth. They offer flexibility and convenience for suitable candidates, allowing patients to remove them when eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing. Consistent wear and professional monitoring are essential for achieving the expected results.

Early Orthodontic Treatment

Children may benefit from an orthodontic evaluation while their teeth and jaws are still developing. Early assessments can help identify potential bite problems, crowding, and jaw development concerns. An orthodontist can determine whether early intervention is necessary or whether monitoring is more appropriate.

What to Expect During Your Orthodontic Consultation

Your first orthodontic consultation is an opportunity to understand your dental needs and discuss possible treatment options. The orthodontic team typically evaluates your teeth, bite alignment, and jaw structure before recommending a suitable approach.

Depending on your situation, your consultation may include dental photographs, digital scans, or X-rays. These assessments help the orthodontist understand your current dental condition and develop an individualised treatment plan.

You can also discuss treatment duration, appointment schedules, maintenance requirements, and available payment arrangements. Asking questions during your consultation helps you feel more confident about moving forward.

Maintaining Your Smile After Orthodontic Treatment

Completing orthodontic treatment is an exciting achievement, but maintaining your results requires ongoing care. Teeth can gradually shift after braces or aligner treatment, making retainers an important part of the retention process.

Your orthodontist may recommend removable or fixed retainers depending on your dental needs. Following the recommended retainer schedule, attending dental check-ups, and maintaining good oral hygiene can help protect your results.

Brushing twice daily, flossing regularly, and limiting sugary foods and drinks also contribute to long-term dental health.
